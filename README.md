## About Me
Hi, I'm Ren 👋

I hold a degree in Cognitive Science with a Computational Emphasis and a minor in Psychology. Between customer-facing roles and academic research, I've learned to pair technical skills with an understanding of human behavior and operational workflow. 

After taking a research course during my academics, I discovered my passion for uncovering hidden patterns behind messy data. My coursework provided me with a solid foundation in programming, which I’ve continued to build on through online courses and personal projects.

This repository aims to showcases my projects and tracks my growth as a data analyst.
